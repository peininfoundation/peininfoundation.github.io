1 分鐘看懂如何使用陪您 · A 1-minute guide to getting started

For Family · 給家人

第一部分：設定（由子女完成）

Part 1: Setup — done once by an adult child, in just a few minutes.

1

下載陪您

Download PéiNín

在您父母的手機上，從 App Store 或 Google Play 下載陪您。搜尋「陪您 PéiNín」即可。

On your parent's phone, download PéiNín from the App Store or Google Play. Search "PéiNín."

2

建立長輩的個人檔案

Set up your parent's profile

回答幾個簡單問題：怎麼稱呼他們、選擇語言（國語 / 廣東話 / 英語）、AI 的語氣和聲音、還有他們感興趣的話題。這些讓陪您更懂您的父母。

Answer a few simple questions — their name, language, the companion's tone and voice, and topics they enjoy.

3

設定緊急聯絡人

Add an emergency contact

填上一位家人的姓名和電話。當長輩按下 SOS，這位家人會立刻收到通知和位置。

Add a family member's name and phone. When your parent presses SOS, this person is alerted instantly with their location.

4

設定提醒（選擇性）

Add reminders (optional)

為長輩加上吃藥、看醫生等提醒。您和長輩都可以隨時新增或修改。如果提醒沒被確認，您會收到通知。

Add reminders for medication or appointments. Both you and your parent can manage them anytime.

完成後，把手機交給您的父母就好。他們不需要記住任何設定 —— 剩下的就是開口說話。
Once setup is done, hand the phone to your parent. They don't need to remember anything — they just talk.
For Seniors · 給長輩

第二部分：每天怎麼用

Part 2: Daily use — this is all your parent ever needs to do.

🐱 🎤
按這裡說話

按一下，就開始說話

Tap once, and just talk

打開陪您，會看到一隻可愛的貓咪。按一下貓咪，就可以開始說話 —— 問問題、聊天、說說今天的心情。

Open PéiNín and you'll see a friendly cat. Tap it, and start talking — ask anything, or just chat.

💬

用您的語言，自然對話

Talk naturally, in your language

用國語或廣東話自然說話就好，就像跟朋友聊天。陪您聽得懂，也會用同樣的語言回答，還會記得您說過的事。

Speak naturally in Mandarin or Cantonese. PéiNín understands, replies in your language, and remembers what you share.

🚨

需要幫忙時，按 SOS

Press SOS if you need help

如果發生緊急情況，按下紅色的 SOS 按鈕，確認後，您的家人會立刻收到通知和您的位置。

In an emergency, press the red SOS button and confirm. Your family is alerted instantly with your location.

就這麼簡單。沒有選單、沒有小按鈕、沒有東西要學。想聊天的時候，按一下貓咪就好。
That's it. No menus, no small buttons, nothing to learn. Whenever they want to talk, they tap the cat.
