Section 1

Introduction

Pei Nin Foundation ("we", "us", or "our") operates the PéiNín (陪您) mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting the privacy and dignity of our users, particularly Chinese-speaking seniors in the United States.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal information. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Section 2

Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Name, age, and preferred language (Mandarin or Cantonese)
  • Emergency contact information (family member name and phone number)
  • Account credentials (email and password, managed by your family member)
  • Voice recordings during conversations with the AI companion
  • Text messages and conversation history

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device type and operating system
  • App usage data (features used, session duration)
  • GPS location data (only when location sharing is enabled by the user or family)
  • Log data and error reports

2.3 Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect Social Security numbers or government ID numbers
  • We do not collect financial or payment information directly (payments processed by third-party providers)
  • We do not sell your personal information to third parties
Section 3

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide AI-powered voice companionship in Mandarin and Cantonese
  • Send emergency SOS alerts to designated family members
  • Share GPS location with designated family members when enabled
  • Remember conversation history to provide personalized companionship
  • Send daily reminders and wellness check-ins
  • Improve the App and our services
  • Comply with legal obligations
Section 4

Voice Data and AI Conversations

Voice recordings are processed to enable natural language conversation with our AI companion. We use trusted third-party AI service providers to process voice and text data. These providers are required to handle your data securely and are prohibited from using your data for their own purposes.

Conversation history is stored securely and is accessible only to the senior user and their designated family members. We do not use your conversations to train AI models without your explicit consent.

Section 5

Location Data

GPS location data is only collected when location sharing is explicitly enabled by the user or their designated family member. Location data is shared only with designated family members and is never sold or shared with third parties. You may disable location sharing at any time in the App settings.

Section 6

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information in the following limited circumstances:

  • With designated family members as authorized by the user
  • With trusted service providers who help us operate the App (AI processing, cloud storage, SMS services)
  • When required by law or to protect the safety of our users
  • In the event of a merger or acquisition, with appropriate notice to users
Section 7

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including encryption of data in transit and at rest. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to use strong passwords and to contact us immediately if you suspect unauthorized access to your account.

Section 8

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at info@peininfoundation.org. We will delete your data within 30 days of your request, except where retention is required by law.

Section 9

Children's Privacy

The PéiNín App is designed for adults aged 60 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe we have collected information from a minor, please contact us immediately.

Section 10

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to access — you may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Right to correction — you may request correction of inaccurate information
  • Right to deletion — you may request deletion of your personal information
  • Right to opt-out — you may opt out of certain data processing activities

To exercise these rights, please contact us at info@peininfoundation.org.

Section 11

California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to delete your personal information, and the right to opt out of the sale of your personal information. We do not sell personal information. To exercise your California privacy rights, contact us at info@peininfoundation.org.

Section 12

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services to operate:

  • AI language processing services — for Mandarin and Cantonese voice conversation
  • Cloud storage services — for secure data storage
  • SMS services — for emergency SOS alerts to family members
  • Payment processors — for subscription management

Each of these services has their own privacy policies. We select partners who meet high standards for data security and privacy.

Section 13

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by sending an email to your registered email address or by displaying a prominent notice in the App. Your continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

Section 14

Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us. We are committed to responding to all privacy inquiries within 30 days.
