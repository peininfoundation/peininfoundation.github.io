Account & Data Deletion

刪除您的帳戶與資料

Delete Your Account & Data
for PéiNín - AI Companion for Seniors · an app by PéiNín Foundation

您隨時有權刪除您的帳戶，以及所有相關的個人資料。您可以直接在 App 內刪除，或寄信給我們。這個頁面說明兩種方法、哪些資料會被刪除，以及處理需要多久。

You may delete your account and all associated personal data at any time — either directly in the app or by emailing us. This page explains both methods, what data is deleted, and how long it takes.

方法一：在 App 內刪除（最快）Method 1: Delete In-App (Fastest)

您可以直接在陪您 App 內刪除帳戶，即時生效：

開啟 App，前往「設定」 Open the app and go to Settings

在陪您 App 中，點選「設定」。
In the PéiNín app, tap Settings.

選擇「刪除帳戶」 Tap "Delete Account"

在設定中選擇「刪除帳戶」，並確認。您的帳戶與相關個人資料將被刪除。
Tap Delete Account and confirm. Your account and associated personal data will be deleted.

方法二：寄信給我們Method 2: Email Us

如果您無法登入 App，或偏好由我們代為處理，也可以寄信給我們：

寄一封電郵給我們 Email us your request

寄信到 info@peininfoundation.org，主旨寫「刪除帳戶 / Delete My Account」。下方的按鈕會自動幫您填好。
Email info@peininfoundation.org with the subject "Delete My Account." The button below fills this in for you.

註明您的帳戶資訊 Include your account details

在信中寫上您註冊時使用的電郵或顯示名稱，讓我們核實您的身份。
Include the email address or display name you registered with, so we can verify your identity.

我們核實並刪除 We verify and delete

我們核實後會處理刪除，並在完成後回覆確認。整個過程在 30 天內完成。
We verify your request, delete your data, and email you a confirmation once complete — within 30 days.

哪些資料會被刪除What Data Is Deleted

刪除請求處理完成後，以下資料將被永久刪除或匿名化：

  • 帳戶與個人資料 · Account and profile — name, email, language, interests
  • 對話紀錄與摘要 · Conversation history and summaries
  • 語音生成音檔 · AI-generated audio files
  • 提醒與用藥資料 · Reminders and medication schedules
  • 緊急聯絡人資訊 · Emergency contact information
  • 活動與參與紀錄 · Engagement and activity data
  • 位置資料 · Stored location data

哪些資料會保留，以及保留多久What Data Is Kept, and For How Long

大部分資料在 30 天內刪除。少數資料因法律要求會保留較長時間，且僅用於法律合規：

  • 法律要求的紀錄 · Records we are legally required to keep (e.g. financial or tax records) — retained for up to 7 years as required by U.S. and California law, then deleted.
  • 匿名化統計資料 · Anonymized, aggregated usage data that can no longer identify you — may be kept indefinitely, as it is no longer personal data.

除上述情況外，所有個人資料都會被刪除。
Apart from the above, all personal data is deleted.

處理時間How Long It Takes

我們會盡快處理刪除請求。完整刪除將在 30 天內完成。刪除的對話紀錄無法復原。

⏱️ 30 天內完成刪除 · Completed within 30 days

We process deletion requests promptly. Full deletion is completed within 30 days. Deleted conversation history cannot be recovered.

關於家人帳戶連結About Linked Family Accounts

如果您的帳戶與家人的帳戶相連結，刪除您的帳戶只會移除您自己的資料，不會刪除已連結家人的獨立帳戶。若需一併移除連結，請在信中說明。

If your account is linked to a family member's, deleting your account removes only your own data — it does not delete the linked family member's separate account. If you'd like the family link removed too, mention it in your email.

有其他問題？Have Questions?

刪除帳戶前若有任何疑問，歡迎來信 info@peininfoundation.org。 您也可以查看我們的隱私政策了解完整的資料處理方式。

If you have questions before deleting, email info@peininfoundation.org. You can also review our Privacy Policy for full details on how we handle data.
