Section 2

Description of Service

PéiNín is an AI-powered companion application designed for Chinese-speaking seniors in the United States. The App provides:

  • AI voice companionship in Mandarin and Cantonese
  • Emergency SOS alerts to designated family members
  • GPS location sharing with designated family members
  • Daily reminders and wellness check-ins
  • Assistance navigating American daily life in Chinese
Important: PéiNín is a companion and assistance tool. It is NOT a medical device, emergency response service, or substitute for professional medical, legal, or emergency services.
